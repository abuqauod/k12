Two roles

For school records (students, families, staff, grades, fees, health notes), the school is in charge of the data (the controller) and we process it on its behalf. Questions about those records, including requests to see or correct them, go to the school; we help the school answer them. The Data Processing Agreement sets out how.

For our own customers (the people who sign up, school owners and billing contacts), we are the controller. This policy mainly describes that data.

What we collect about our customers

  • Sign-up details: school name, your name, email, phone, country and approximate number of students.
  • Account data: sign-in times, sessions, and actions in the audit log.
  • Billing data: invoices and payments. Card details are entered on the payment provider's page (PayTabs or HyperPay); we never see or store card numbers.
  • Technical data needed to run the Service safely: IP addresses in rate limits and error reports.

Why we use it

To provide and bill for the Service, to send service messages (invitations, password resets, invoices, trial and renewal reminders), to keep the Service secure, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data or use school records for advertising.
