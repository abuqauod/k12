1. Roles and scope

The School is the controller of the personal data it enters into the Service; [Company legal name] is the processor. This agreement covers that data: students, parents and guardians, staff, applicants, and the records about them (attendance, grades, fees, health, behaviour, documents).

2. Instructions

We process the data only to provide the Service as the School configures and uses it, and on its other documented instructions, unless the law requires otherwise (in which case we tell the School first where we may).

3. Confidentiality

Only our staff who need access to support or run the Service have it, under confidentiality obligations, and access is logged.

4. Security measures

  • Every request is checked against the user's school, role and campus; one school's records cannot be read from another's account.
  • Encryption in transit (TLS); gateway credentials encrypted at rest; passwords hashed.
  • Audit log of changes; rate limits on sign-in and public endpoints.
  • Daily backups, kept for [n] days, and tested restores.
  • Health details are visible only to roles the School grants health access to; only the items the School marks as alerts are shown to all staff who can see the student.

5. Sub-processors

The School authorises the sub-processors listed in the Privacy Policy. We give 30 days' notice before adding or replacing one; the School may object, and if we cannot address the objection, end the subscription with a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.

6. Data location and transfers

Data is stored in [region]. Where it is transferred abroad, we do so only under the safeguards the applicable law requires. Schools that must keep data in their own country can ask for [in-country hosting option / Enterprise].

7. Helping the School

We help the School respond to requests from parents, staff and students to see, correct or delete their data, and with impact assessments and questions from authorities, as far as the Service allows.

8. Breaches

We tell the School without undue delay, and within 72 hours of becoming aware, of a breach affecting its data, with what we know and what we are doing, and keep it informed.

9. End of the service

After the subscription ends the School can export its data for 60 days; we then delete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it, and backups are overwritten on their normal cycle. On request we confirm deletion in writing.

10. Audits

We make available the information needed to show compliance with this agreement, and allow reasonable audits once a year on 30 days' notice, at the School's cost.
